Responsive Tables

The .table-responsive class creates a responsive table. The table will then scroll horizontally on small devices(under 768px). When viewing on anything larger than 768px wide, there is no diffrence

# Fname Lname Age City Country
1 Sahil Bhalani 21 New York United States Of America
